Generated by installing HaikuOS in Hyper-V on a 32 GB IDE disk with a main 30 GB OS part
and a secondary 4 MB partition, both formatted to BeFS during OS installation), then
mounting both the 4 MB partition and the iso9660.iso with the EFI files, copying the files
and then mounting the VM's .vhdx in Windows and using HxD to extract the 4 MB partition
(after getting its start sector and size from Rufus).
